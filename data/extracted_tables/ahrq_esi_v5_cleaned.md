## PHẦN 1: THUẬT TOÁN PHÂN LUỒNG ESI (ESI TRIAGE ALGORITHM LOGIC)
*(Trích xuất và chuẩn hóa từ Figure 2-1, 2-2 và các Text blocks)*

*   **Decision Point A:** Requires immediate lifesaving intervention?
    *   **Yes** ➔ **ESI 1**
    *   **No** ➔ Move to B
*   **Decision Point B:** High-risk situation? OR Confused/lethargic/disoriented? OR Severe pain/distress?
    *   **Yes** ➔ **ESI 2**
    *   **No** ➔ Move to C
*   **Decision Point C:** How many different resources are needed?
    *   **None** ➔ **ESI 5**
    *   **One** ➔ **ESI 4**
    *   **Many** ➔ Move to D
*   **Decision Point D:** High-risk vital signs? (Reassess acuity)
    *   **Yes** ➔ Upgrade/Consider **ESI 2**
    *   **No** ➔ **ESI 3**

## PHẦN 2: GIẢI THÍCH CHI TIẾT CÁC ĐIỂM QUYẾT ĐỊNH (DECISION POINTS)

**A. Immediate lifesaving intervention required:** 
Airway or respiratory support, emergency medications, hemodynamic interventions such as fluid resuscitation or blood products. Clinical presentations requiring lifesaving interventions include the following: intubated, unresponsive, pulselessness, apneic, severe respiratory distress, profound hypotension or hypoglycemia. 
*Unresponsiveness is defined as a patient who either:*
1. Is nonverbal and not following commands (acutely) OR 
2. Requires noxious stimulus (P or U on AVPU scale).

**B. High-risk situation:** 
May become unstable, have high risk for deterioration, or exhibit newly altered mental status. Severe pain or distress is determined by patient report, corroborated with clinical observation.

**C. Resources:** 
Count the number of *different types* of resources, not the individual tests or radiographs. *(For example: complete blood count, electrolytes, and coagulant studies = 1 resource because they are all lab tests. CBC + chest radiograph = 2 resources).*

**D. High-risk vital signs:** 
Reassess to determine whether the patient warrants a higher acuity level if a patient has one or more vital signs outside the normal parameters for the patient.

| :--- | :--- |
